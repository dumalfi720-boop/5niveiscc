MODULE 2.3 · COMPLETE CASES · ~45 min

🎯 Real Project Cases + Connectors + Memory

3 complete assemblies of Project N2, each with the initial wrong configuration, exact adjustment that unlocked, final standard prompt that becomes a weekly ritual, tested variations and real metrics.

⚠ Different from N1.In N2 the construction is in two layers:Project configuration(fixed instruction + docs + connectors) anddefault prompt(what you re-run). The cases show both — if you get the configuration wrong, the best prompt in the world won't save you.

CASE 1

💼 "Outbound Sales B2B" project — from generic pitch to first scheduled meeting

Scenario

Felipe is an AE in a financial management SaaS for medical clinics. ICP is a clinic with 5-20 doctors in Brazil. Goal: 4 meetings/week via cold outbound. Today he gets a 1.5 on average because his emails are "very junior SDR standard".

Available resources:detailed ICP doc (Drive), 12 emails that held meetings in the last 6 months (Gmail), Calendar with open times.

❌ Initial setup (what does NOT work)

Project:"Sales"
Fixed instruction:"you are my sales copywriter, help me write emails"
Connectors:none activated
Docs:nothing attached

Typical output:Grammarly correct email, totally generic ("I saw that your clinic has grown, we want to help you..."), exactly what junior SDR writes. Answer: 1.5%.

Why it failed:

  • • "Sales" project is too generic — Claude doesn't know which ICP to write to
  • • Without ICP doc, Claude invents pain that may not exist in the clinic
  • • Without samples of the 12 emails that worked, Claude does not learn the tone Felipe
  • • Without Gmail connector, Felipe copies and pastes all context information

✅ Final configuration that becomes a weekly ritual

Project:"Outbound Clínicas Médicas BR"

Active Connectors:Google Drive ("outbound clinics" folder), Gmail (label "sales-ganhas-2026")

Docs attached to knowledge:

  • • icp-clinicas-medicas-v3.pdf— ideal customer profile
  • • 12-emails-ganhos-com-anotacoes.md— those who held a meeting + analysis of why
  • • objecoes-comuns-resolvidas.md— top 8 objections with answers
  • • linguagem-veto.md— “automate”, “save time”, “transform management” — obvious words that MARK junior SDR

Fixed Project Instruction:

You are Felipe, senior AE of financial management SaaS for medical clinics. You write cold outbound that sounds consultative, not salesy. Whenever I ask for an email: 1. Read the ICP doc first — confirm the recipient qualifies (if not, decline and let me know) 2. Re-read 3 of the 12 winning emails that seem closest to the context 3. Use these tones — don’t invent a new tone 4. Check the veto language: if it says "automate", "save time", "transform management", "leverage results", REWRITE 5. Don’t promise a generic demonstration — propose a specific conversation about 1 concrete ICP pain point Format always: - Subject (max 7 words, without "[company]" at the beginning — cliché) - Body (max 90 words) - CTA (a concrete question, not "can we talk?") Whenever I say "analysis mode", look at recent Gmail emails and tell me pattern of who responded vs who didn't. Don't guess, just use the data.

📤 Standard weekly ritual prompt

List of prospects of the week below. For each one, write the email following the project instructions. Before each email, in 1 line: "because this lead fits the ICP" + "which win-email is closest". Prospects: - Dr. Mariana Silva — Cardio+ Clinic, 8 doctors, São Paulo. LinkedIn says it will grow 40% in 2025. Website shows that they use an Excel spreadsheet for their calendars. - Dr. Ricardo Oliveira — Beta Medical Center, 14 doctors, Curitiba. Local news: they received funding. Professional website, looks organized. - [...]

📤 Example output (1 of the 6 emails generated)

ICP Fitting:Cardio+ has 8 doctors (target range), 40% growth signals scalable process pain. Closest earnings email: that of Clínica DermaNova (grew 35%, they also used Excel, closed in 2 calls).

Subject:Schedule in Excel when to double the team

Dr. Mariana,

I saw that Cardio+ grew 40% in 2025 — congratulations. I recently spoke to a clinic in Belo Horizonte that was similar in size and grew at a similar pace; The first thing that got in the way for them was that the Excel diary could no longer accommodate 4 secretaries editing together.

I didn't come to offer a demonstration. I have a short spreadsheet with the calculation of when this limit usually exceeds (based on 38 clinics). Can I send you?

Philip
[enterprise]

Unlike "I saw that your clinic has grown, we want to help you automate...". It has specific pain, low commitment offer (spreadsheet > demo), closed CTA.

🔀 4 variations tested

A — Lead responded with objection:"objection mode: let's check objecoes-comuns-resolvidas.md, identify which one it is, and respond in the same tone as the won-emails".
B — Email follow-up with no response (7 days):Gmail connector searches the original email, identifies unanswered point, generates follow-up that DOES NOT repeat the pitch — adds new value.
C — Inbound (lead arrived via website):same instruction, but swaps “cold lead” for “warm lead”, changes CTA to “can I send you?” to "which of these 3 times works?".
D — Monthly pipeline analysis:"analysis mode: Gmail search, compares response rate by sector, day of the week, time. Suggests 2 adjustments for next month".

⚠ Common mistakes when adapting

  • • Skip the "emails gained from annotations" doc— without that, Claude invents tone. Notes explaining “why this one worked” are worth 10x more than just raw emails.
  • • Forget the veto language— sales clichés come back immediately. Explicit list is mandatory.
  • • Don't use the Gmail connector for "analysis mode"— those who only use it to generate emails lose 50% of the value of the Project.
  • • "Sales" project instead of "Outbound Clínicas Médicas BR"— wide scope is poison in N2.

CASE 2

📸 "Instagram Content" project — brand voice that sounds human

Scenario

Júlia owns an artisanal natural cosmetics brand (sells via Instagram, ~9k followers). Posts 3x/week. There is no budget for an agency. Today he spends 4 hours/week creating posts — tone oscillates between "X product with Y benefit" (seller) and "sunny morning and gratitude" (motivational templates). Engagement stagnated at 3.2%.

Features:200+ old posts on IG, brand book PDF (written by designer 2 years ago), audio recordings of Júlia explaining products (authentic).

❌ Initial Setup

Project:"Marketing"
Instruction:"you are my social media, you write posts for IG"
Knowledge:just the brand book

Output:generically "instagrammable" posts, too many emojis, "✨ nature blesses us..." — a voice that sounds like it comes from any natural cosmetic brand.

Why it failed:brand book PDF is toneaspirationalwritten by designer; Júlia's REAL voice (authentic, technical, angry with greenwashing) is in the audios and in the posts received — which no one attached.

✅ Final configuration

Project:"IG @marca-natural — Júlia's real voice"

Attached knowledge (in order of importance):

  • • top-20-posts-engajamento.md— the 20 with the best performance, with a note from Júlia: "this one is the voice I want forever"
  • • transcricao-audios-julia.md— Júlia explaining 8 products via audio, transcribed without cutting
  • • tabu-list.md— "✨", "sunny morning", "energy", "alchemy", "made with love" — everything you NEVER use
  • • brand-book.pdf— secondary reference, not primary
  • • linhas-de-produtos-2026.md— actual technical data sheet of each item

Fixed instruction:

You are Júlia writing. It’s not “brand social media.” IT'S SHE. Every time I request a post: 1. Reread 5 random posts from the top-20 and the audio transcription 2. Capture the REAL tone: direct, technical, slightly angry at market lies 3. Check the taboo list — if any words come out, REWRITE 4. DO NOT use decorative emoji. Technical emoji (📦 → shipping, ⚠ → attention) ok. 5. Don't end with "💚" / "✨" / "🌿" Standard post format: - Hook in the first line (max 8 words, generates doubt or counterintuitive) - 2-3 short paragraphs (max 4 lines each) - 1 proof (number, name, verifiable fact) - 1 clear action at the end (not "💚") Whenever I say "critical mode", look at the last post I published and tell me in 3 bullets what was NOT in Júlia's voice.

📤 Output example

Hook:"Natural cosmetic" does not mean it is good for the skin.

The industry has discovered that this label sells. Today there are products with 3% natural ingredients and 97% basic chemicals that declare themselves "natural" without technically lying.

The real difference is in the list — not the label, not the vibe of the packaging.

Our facial moisturizer has 14 ingredients. 13 are natural identified in INCI. The 14th is a certified natural preservative (without it, fungus in 5 days).

Want to see the entire list before deciding? Today's Stories have.

— Julia

Júlia's voice, specific test (14 ingredients), real CTA (stories). Engagement for this post: 8.4%. Authentic voice converts more than “product X with benefit Y”.

🔀 4 variations tested

A — Carousel (8 slides):same tone, but divided. Slide 1 = expanded hook, slides 2-7 = one point each, slide 8 = CTA.
B — Reel (script):"reel mode": 30 seconds, first phrase is a 3s hook, suggested visual action, without motivational music.
C — Sequenced Stories (5):tells the story in 5 slides with a cliffhanger for the next one.
D — Launch of new product:use technical sheetlinhas-de-produtosand comes across Júlia's voice. It doesn't become a press release.

⚠ Errors when adapting

  • • Rely on the brand book as the primary source of voice— brand book is aspirational, real voice is in posts/audios. Use brand book as a guardrail, not as a map.
  • • Forget the taboo list— Instagram clichés are gravitational. If you don't veto it, they come back.
  • • Not noting "why this post worked" in the top-20— without annotation, Claude copies form but does not capture intention.
  • • Skip "critical mode"— feedback on what COMES OUT is what sets the tone in the long term.

CASE 3

🧠 Customer service with Memory + Search old chats

Scenario

Rafa owns a boutique paid traffic agency (5 employees, 18 clients). Serves customers mainly via WhatsApp and Slack. Every week he has to re-explain to the team: "remember client Y? we decided not to use Performance Max because it has a luxury ICP, but the team forgets and the campaign appears duplicated".

Features:6 months of conversations on Slack #cliente-Y, drive with recorded decisions, Notion with playbooks.

❌ Initial Setup

Project:"Customers"
Connectors:none
Memory:disabled
Knowledge:1 giant PDF "all customers"

Output:mixed answers — when Rafa asks about client Y, data from client W comes up. Giant PDF = Claude is confusing.

Why it failed:1 project for 18 clients is chaos. Memory off = re-explain everything with each new chat. Without a Slack/Drive connector, Rafa pastes context manually.

✅ Final configuration (rule of thumb: 1 project = 1 client)

Projects:18 — one per customer. Name: "Traffic — [client] — ICP/restriction on 1 line". Ex.: "Traffic — Luxo Watches — ICP HNW, NEVER use PMax".

Connectors by design:Slack (client specific channel), Drive (client folder), Notion (client playbook). Everything hidden.

Memory:activated with explicit rules — "always remember: client Y never uses PMax, ICP is luxury, restriction is budget < R$50k/month".

Fixed instruction (same template for 18, changing 3 fields):

Client: [NAME] ICP: [DESCRIPTION in 1 sentence] Non-negotiable restrictions: [LIST] You are the senior account manager responsible for this client. Know the history of decisions, know what we have tried and discarded. Before answering any questions: 1. Check Slack history #client-[NAME] for the last 90 days 2. Check decisions recorded in Drive/decisoes-cliente-[NAME].md 3. Check playbook in Notion/client-[NAME] 4. If the question touches on something already decided, CITE the decision and when it was made 5. NEVER suggest something on the list of non-negotiable restrictions without warning When I ask a new question, save the decision in memory so that the next conversation does not lose context. /summary mode: generate monthly customer reports — performance, decisions, next steps.

📤 Output example

Rafa's question:"We are thinking about testing Performance Max on Luxo Watches to scale. Does that make sense?"

🚨 Attention: PMax is on this client's list of NON-NEGOTIABLE restrictions.

Original decision (Slack #luxo-watches, 14/Feb/2026): you decided to veto PMax here because HNW ICP (high-net-worth) responds poorly to mass positioning, and PMax optimizes for volume. Bruno (from the client's team) had brought this preference to the board.

If the intention is to scale, alternatives that fit the veto:
1. Demand Gen (intent-based segmentation) — aligned with ICP, we saw a small test in Mar/26 with acceptable CPA
2. Search with premium keywords + manual bidding — more control, avoids loose matching
3. YouTube with manual placement on aligned channels (Forbes, Bloomberg) — more expensive option but aligns image

Do you want me to detail the budget and ramp-up of one of these 3? Or is there a new reason to reopen the PMax veto?

See: Claude CITES the original decision, highlights the restriction, offers aligned alternatives, and opens the door to reopen the veto if there is reason. This is a coworker, not a search engine.

🔀 4 variations tested

A — New employee onboarding:"briefing mode: new team member takes on client X. Generate onboarding in 1 page with key decisions, restrictions and contacts".
B — Contract renewal:"renewal mode: generate renewal proposal with demonstrated ROI for the last 12 months, risks if we leave, and 3 upgrade options".
C — Crisis (campaign broke):"incident mode: customer complained at 10pm. Summarize what we know, 3 probable causes, immediate action in the morning".
D — Monthly for client:"report mode: monthly report for the client. Tom: confident, without excusing bad results. Include the next 3 tests".

⚠ Errors when adapting

  • • 1 project for many customers— guaranteed chaos. 1 project = 1 client is the N2 rule of thumb when working on an account basis.
  • • Memory disabled— disabled on purpose or by forgetfulness, is the ingredient that most reduces repetitive "re-explanation".
  • • Do not write NON-NEGOTIABLE restrictions in the project name— Rafa puts it in his name ("NEVER PMax") because it's what he forgets the most. Memory helps, but project name is extra fail-safe.
  • • Do not use Slack connector— without it, you paste conversations manually. Connector reduces 80% of copy-paste.

============ ABSTRACTED PATTERN ============
★

🧠 The N2 standard behind the 3 cases

Note that the 3 cases have identical anatomy. This is the formula that separates Project that works from Project that is undead:

  1. 1. Surgical scope in the project name— "Outbound Clínicas Médicas BR", not "Sales". "IG @marca-natural — real voice Júlia", not "Marketing". The name is the first mental filter.
  2. 2. Knowledge layers in importance— top-20 posts > brand book. Earned emails > ICP doc. Real decisions > generic playbook. Attaching everything in the same order doesn't work; prioritize.
  3. 3. Taboo-list/explicit language-veto— listing what to NEVER use is more effective than listing what to use. Cliches have gravity; veto in writing.
  4. 4. Connectors SCOPE to the project— Slack from the right channel, Drive from the right folder. Too wide connector causes noise.
  5. 5. Named modes (/summary, /analysis, /critique, /briefing)— you teach Claude to deliver different formats of the same config. Multiplies reuse.

These 5 survive in any sector. Decorate them and your next project, in any area, will be ready in 30 minutes instead of 3 hours.
