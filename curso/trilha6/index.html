📜 How it works

  • • 10 questions per level— 50 in total. Each with 4 alternatives, 1 correct.
  • • Score by level— you see how many you got right in each of the 5.
  • • Rule:your current level = the HIGHEST you got ≥8/10 right. Ex.: 9 hits in N1+N2+N3, 6 in N4, 2 in N5 → you are in N3.
  • • Commented feedback— at the end, each wrong question comes with an explanation and link to the track module it covers.
  • • No login— score is only in your browser. You can do it as many times as you want.

⚠ Before you start — read

Don't cheat on yourself.The goal isn't to "get through" — it's to find out where you really are. Anyone who pastes the questionnaire into ChatGPT and pastes the answer loses 100% of the value.
