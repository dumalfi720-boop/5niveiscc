💼 What to sell and how to find out what already exists

N5's most leveraged skill isn't building from scratch — it's discovering what the community has already done and adapting it to the client's context.

1

📚 Community catalog (5k+ skills, 800+ MCPs, 3k+ marketplaces)

In May/2026 the ecosystem already has more than5,000 skills published, 800+ MCP servers, 3,000+ marketplacesof plugins. Mostly open-source, a lot of mature stuff.

Anyone who enters N5 thinking they need to create everything from scratch is wasting 80% of the time. Whoever mines first delivers the customer in 1 week instead of 1 month.

2

🔍 Where to mine

  • • Official Anthropic Marketplace— Categorized, Verified, Secure
  • • GitHub— search for "awesome-claude-code", "awesome-claude-skills"
  • • X (Twitter)— "claude code skill" + your vertical (devops/sales/finance/legal)
  • • Reddit— r/ClaudeAI, r/LocalLLaMA have threads of real implementations
  • • Vertical marketplaces— some just devops, others just sales, etc.
3

🔧 How to adapt OSS without becoming a hostage

Discipline to avoid being stuck with the old version:

  1. 1.Fork before moving — don't edit the original
  2. 2.Document each customization in local CHANGELOG.md
  3. 3.Periodic upstream rebase (monthly) — catches updates
  4. 4.Contribute patches back when it makes general sense
  5. 5.If the customization becomes greater than the original, it is a sign of creating it from scratch

⚠ Errors that destroy customer trust

❌ Enable routine without dry-run

Customer sees bug first, loses faith.

❌ Sell autonomy in creative tasks

Copywriting/positioning DO NOT automate.

❌ No log monitor

Silent drift destroys quality.

❌ SLA without jurisdiction

"24/7" without defining what scales to humans becomes a problem.

❌ Ignore token cost

Margin disappears without you noticing.

❌ Deliver without runbook

The customer doesn't know how to operate when you disappear.
