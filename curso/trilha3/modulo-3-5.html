MODULE 3.5 · WALKTHROUGH · ~55 min

🎬 From freelancer charging R$120/h to system sold for R$12k + recurring

Follow Bruno in 14 days: from the uncomfortable meeting with Dr. Carla (owner of a dental clinic) to signing the contract. You see the pricing-reflex that would cost 60% of the real value, the discovery that discovered the hidden pain of R$4.4k/month, and the MVP built in 1 day that turned into recurring R$13.8k/year.

⚠ This walkthrough is about SELLING, not building.The technical construction has already been covered in 3.4. Here the focus is on the commercial movement that separates "hourly" freelancers from system providers. You leave knowing how to price.

SETUP
⚙

Setup — Monday, 2pm

Who:Bruno, 31, freelance dev for 5 years. Creates CRUDs, dashboards, small integrations. Charges R$120/hour — earns R$8-10k/month, largely depends on the volume of the week. You're tired of this rollercoaster.

The opportunity:met Dr. Carla, dentist, owner of the Sorri+ clinic (3 dentists, 1 receptionist, 1 assistant) 6 months ago — Bruno created her website. Today Carla sent WhatsApp:

"Bruno, are you available for coffee this week? I need to organize the reception's life, she's overwhelmed. Maybe you can help."

What Bruno knows (and still doesn't know):

  • • Do you know that Carla's receptionist, Joana, spends a lot of time answering patients
  • • You know that Carla is "the type who trusts whoever delivers" — she won't haggle much if the proposal makes sense
  • • Don't knowAlso: Joana's 4 hours a day are spent sorting messages, costing R$ 4.4k/month in wasted time
  • • Don't know: the temptation to charge R$4.8k (40 hours × R$120) will appear and he will have to resist
ITER 0
0

Attempt 0 — pricing-reflex (Monday night)

Bruno, excited, opens the Claude before arranging coffee with Carla. He wants to “do the math on how much to charge”.

BRUNO →
Customer will request automation of message sorting from reception. I think it can be done in 30-40 hours of work. How much to charge?
← CLAUDE
At R$120/hour × 35h = **R$4,200**. Reasonable range would be R$3.5k-R$5k, depending on the actual complexity.

😬 This is pricing-reflex — and it costs 60% of the real value

Bruno was satisfied with the answer. I would propose R$4.5k to Carla on Wednesday. It would CLOSE. It would deliver in 1 month. And I would have lostR$7.5k in real value.

Why the "hours × rate" reflex is bad:

  • • Prices the INPUTS (your time), not the RESULT (pain that the client takes away)
  • • Treats you as a replaceable input, not a solution provider
  • • Does not capture recurring value — you deliver and disappear, customer does not pay for maintenance
  • • Does not create a barrier for the “cheaper per hour” competitor

LESSONS
★

Lessons from the journey

1. Pricing-reflex is the biggest limitation of N3

“Hours × rate” sounds honest, but it’s what keeps you freelance. The right question: “how much is it worth to resolve this for the customer?” — not “how much does it cost me to do it?”. Change the starting point of the account.

2. Quantitative discovery before proposal > after

Bruno arrived at the cafe with a diagnosis of R$4k/month. Carla did not negotiate because HER mathematics confirmed the proposal. Without discovery, he would have proposed R$4.2k and she would have "closed satisfied" — both losing.

3. Recurring is worth 3-5x the setup

R$1,800/month × 12 = R$21,600. More than the setup. And it doesn’t ask for new prospecting. It's what takes freelancers off the cash flow rollercoaster.

4. Give the system a NAME

"Smile+ Intelligent Service" is easy to send to another business owner. "The automation that Bruno created" is not. Name = product. Referenceable product.

5. Include training of the “threatened” person

Joana could have sabotaged the system ("I made a mistake so Carla could see that it didn't work"). Training Joana became an ally — she got a raise for being a “system supervisor.” Always identify who can boycott and bring them along.

ADAPTATIONS
⚙

Adapt to your market

The "hourly → recurring system" movement works in any sector where there is measurable and repetitive pain:

🏥 Health — other clinics

Replicate the Sorri+ case to pediatricians, physios, psychologists. Each vertical with its specific templates. R$ 8-15k setup + R$ 1.2-2k/month.

🛒 E-commerce — SAC screening

Stores with medium volume (200-2000 tickets/month). Setup R$ 10-20k + R$ 2-3k/month. Measurable ROI in service capacity.

🏠 Real estate — lead qualification

Broker spends 4-6h/day separating “curious” from “real buyer”. Setup R$8-12k + R$1.5k/month.

⚖ Law firms — initial screening

Small offices waste time explaining “we can’t handle this”. Setup R$ 15-25k (highest compliance) + R$ 2.5-4k/month.
