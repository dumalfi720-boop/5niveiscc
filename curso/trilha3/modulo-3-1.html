🖥 Co-work: Claude on his machine

The 7 features that take Claude out of a browser tab and put him to work on his machine, with real access to the filesystem, reusable skills, scheduling and control via cell phone.

1

🏠 What is co-working

Co-work is a tab within Claude Desktop that runs code in an isolated VM — but with real reading/writing in the folders you have authorized. You describe the result ("organize my Downloads by type and date"), Claude discovers the steps and executes it.

It differs from chat: chat reasons about what you paste. Co-work acts on your machine. This is the first time that Claude stops being an “assistant who guides you” and becomes an “operator who executes”.

⚠ Important:co-work is paid (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise). If you are on free, this is the signal to evaluate an upgrade.

2

📂 Filesystem access securely

You choose folder by folder what Claude can read and what he can write. Everything outside the scope is invisible. Principle of least privilege: give only what is necessary for the task.

  • • Read-onlyby default — write is explicit opt-in
  • • Folder-by-folder— without "all /Users"
  • • Exclusion list— hide sensitive subfolders
  • • Confirmation before deleting— mandatory rule in your instructions
3

🧩 Reusable skills

Skills are reusable workflows in a markdown file. You define"weekly customer report"once and invokes with 1 command. 100+ published — 16+ Anthropic officials plus the community.

---
name: weekly-client-report
description: Summarizes tickets, sales and NPS for the week
---

# Steps
1. Read entries from 03_entry/
2. Compile key metrics
3. Generate markdown in 05_outputs/relatorio-{data}.md

They work in co-work, chat and Claude Code. Be careful with the origin of community skills — read before installing.

4

⏰ Scheduled tasks (/schedule)

In any co-work conversation,/schedulesaves the task to run at the cadence you define: daily 8 am, weekly Monday, monthly day 1.

Caveat: machine turned on and desktop open. For a 24/7 routine (without a machine), go to N5 (Routines).

Perfect cases for /schedule:daily stand-up from PRs, brief before the meeting reading agenda+docs, email summary 5pm.

5

📱 Control via cell phone (Dispatch)

Pair cell phone with desktop. Send a task for traffic, the gym, the meeting. Claude runs it on the desktop and notifies you when it's finished.

That's when co-work stops being "Claude that I use" and becomes "Claude that works for me". The psychological leap is big — for the first time you "delegate" without typing context.

6

🎨Claude Design

Separate product from Anthropic Labs, included in Pro. You describe it in natural language (deck, landing, one-pager) and Claude delivers + design system extracted from the brand.

The difference: paste your repo, brand book or design files — Claude extracts colors, typography, spacing and uses it in EVERYTHING later. Output doesn't look like "AI slop".

Handoff: package ready for Claude Code (N4), Canva or export ZIP. Non-coder goes from briefing to published website without writing 1 line.

7

🖱 Computer Use

For apps that don't have a native connector, Claude navigates visually like a human: click, type, tab, submit. Mac and Windows.

Use aslast choice: slow, expensive in tokens, more wrong than connectors. Good for intranets, legacy ERPs and proprietary apps.
