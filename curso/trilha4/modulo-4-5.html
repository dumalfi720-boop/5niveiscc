LESSONS
★

Lessons from the journey

1. Friday night shift = "confirm and sleep"

Bug P1 outside "knock-it-all" window doesn't require work at 11pm. Confirm receipt, communicate plan, rest. Clear decision AVOIDS poor judgment due to sleep.

2. Plan mode in ambiguous bug = investigate with method

Mariana didn't ask to "fix the bug". It asked "reproduce, read logs, cross with PRs, form hypothesis with %, fix plan, test plan". 18 minutes later she had a complete map.

3. Parallel subagents = simultaneous audit and fix

"audit-similar" discovered 2 latent bugs that Mariana did NOT plan to look for. They became a separate, pre-emptive ticket. Customer is protected without bloated PR.

4. Verification loop gets you out of "hope-CI"

Claude validated everything before the PR. When CTO opened it on his cell phone, he saw PR with inline evidence — he didn't need to wait for CI to run. Approval in 5 minutes.

5. Proactive communication > quick fix

Feedback from CFO Acme cites “communication BEFORE I charge.” 3 short messages (Friday, Saturday, Sunday) cost 5 minutes in total — transformed the perception of the incident.

ADAPTATIONS
⚙

Adaptations to your context

🔥 P0 that knocks down EVERYTHING in production

Same structure but without "sleeping": Claude operates while you communicate with stakeholders. Plan mode faster (5min, not 18). Verification loop is shorter too.

🐛 "Strange behavior" bug without stack trace

Plan mode investigates: "use verbose log feature flag, replay, join logs, identify line". Without a stack, the hypothesis work is greater.

🚀 Urgent premium customer feature

More elaborate Plan mode (include conflict with in-flight PRs). Verification loop with browser automation for UX. PR slash command with "premium feature" format.

🔁 Refactor affecting 20+ files

Plan mode in 2 steps: 1) maps everything affected 2) groups into logical commits. "consistency-check" subagent after each commit.
