MODULE 4.3 · CHEAT CODE · ~20 min

🚀 Cheat Code: stop drooling Claude

The jump from N4 to N5 is mental, not technical. Most of them stay locked in because they keep "looking" at Claude — this module teaches you how to let go.

1

🍼 Identify what you still do by "looking"

N4 power-users fall into a curious trap: they become babysitters for their own parallel sessions. Trigger 4 sessions, alternate between them, comment on each step. This is babysitting with extra steps.

Audit for 1 week. List 3 tasks that you "did by hand" although they could be slash command, skill, hook or routine. These 3 are your first candidates to become self-employed.

2

🔧 Transform into command, skill or hook

Each candidate goes to one of 3 boxes:

slash command — when it is a prompt shortcut

Ex: /commit-push-pr, /update-deps, /sync-types

Skill — when it is a workflow with steps

Ex: weekly-client-report, bug-screening, generate-sales-deck

Hook — when it is automatic validation at a time

Ex: pre-tool-use (block rm -rf), post-edit (format), stop (notify Slack). Hooks are a teaser for the N5.

3

⚠ Typical Level 4 errors

❌ Manage parallel on the nail

Switch between sessions manually instead of letting /focus + auto do the work.

❌ Skip plan mode

Going straight to execution — generates expensive refactoring.

❌ No verification

Accept first output without self-test.

❌ giant claude.md

>200 lines triggers tokens in every session.

❌ Ignore /insights

No audit = no evolution.

❌ MCP-first

Forget CLI > MCP. Pay 3x in tokens.

4

🚨 Ceiling sign: "I wish it could run without me being here"

You're running /schedule + worktrees + subagents — but you need your laptop to be on for all of this. Then think:"if only it could run the daily PR review without me being online..."

This is the exact ceiling of N4. Answer: cloud routines (N5). There's no way to pierce it — go up.

5

💼 How to charge projects R$25-75k

N4 doesn’t sell isolated automation — it doessystem. Position it like this:

  • • Diagnostics (R$ 5-10k):maps bottlenecks, proposes system. Standalone, generates proposal for the next phase.
  • • Implementation (R$ 25-50k):delivery system with claude.md, skills, slash commands, runbook, training.
  • • Maintenance (R$ 3-8k/month):/monthly insights, adjustments, new features.

Explicit ROI:"I will save your team 2000h/year, X amount. I charge 10%."Customers who understand ROI pay without discounts.

6

📋 Preparing a simple routine for Level 5

Before enabling cloud routines, do pre-work:

  1. 1.Choose 1 very low risk routine.Just for you, not for the client, not external. Ex: "summary of what happened on my team's GitHub, every day at 9 am".
  2. 2.Run manually for 5 days. Refine output until you are satisfied.
  3. 3.Document prompt, input, and expected output.
  4. 4.Set kill switch: "if output exceeds X tokens, fail and let me know".
  5. 5.Only now does it become routine (N5).

Trust builds gradually. Skip = automation that destroys trust on the 1st bug.
